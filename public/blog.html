1. What is Farm Central?

Farm Central (farmcentral.online) is an enterprise-grade, cloud-native Agricultural Command and Commerce Platform designed specifically to overcome structural handicaps faced by smallholder farmers in India and emerging global markets.

In traditional agricultural supply chains, over 85% of farmers suffer from severe information asymmetry: commission agents (arhtiyas) in Agricultural Produce Market Committee (APMC) mandis extract between 6% to 15% in brokerage fees, crop diseases like Panama TR4 and Sigatoka are detected too late, and farmers lack access to precision multi-spectral satellite telemetry.

Farm Central fundamentally dismantles this broken paradigm by creating a single, integrated digital ecosystem connecting agronomic intelligence, real-time climate telemetry, and direct peer-to-peer escrow commerce.

🛰️

NASA Satellite NDVI Telemetry

Enter any farm GPS latitude and longitude to calculate 30-day historical vegetation vigor, solar irradiance, and 7-day soil moisture trends with zero hardware cost.

🛡️

0% Fraud Escrow Trading Hub

Eliminates payment default risks. Buyer funds are locked in an encrypted staging contract until delivery is physically verified, paying farmers directly.

🔬

AI Crop Doctor Pathology

Multi-modal computer vision models analyze leaf photographs to diagnose fungal, bacterial, and pest hazards within seconds, prescribing organic treatments.

📈

Live WebSocket APMC Rates

Real-time mandi price tickers across national commodity markets, enabling farmers to sell harvests at peak market realization.
